The approach

Not a performance. A conversation with the pattern underneath.

Readings are intuitive and symbolic, but the point is not to make your life more mysterious. It is to illuminate what may already be moving through it.

Kathleen’s practice draws on tarot, psychic perception, image, story and conversation. You can arrive with a specific question or simply with the sense that something needs to be looked at differently.

ClarityWhen the situation is noisy but the underlying question is not.
RelationshipsPatterns, dynamics, choices and what is asking to be understood.
TransitionsThreshold moments, endings, beginnings and the territory between them.
Creative & work lifeDirection, confidence, blocks, opportunities and the shape of what comes next.
